# Following a MIDI clock whose pulses stop without a Stop (a Bluetooth dropout, a computer that hangs): the break goes
# on at the clock's tempo (coasting) and rejoins the pulses where the sender is when they come back; a Stop from the
# clock's source, a new Start or PLAY ends it. behind_abs: how far the break is off the clock's line (ticks, 8 a pulse).
#   sh build.sh --host && app/build/host/amenity_host app/tests/stop.txt build/stop
wait 900
midi FA 00 00
clock 96 20
expect engine_playing 1
expect behind_abs <8
