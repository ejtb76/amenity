Add a break

A WAV (or any file your browser plays) of exactly one or two bars: it starts on the first beat and ends where the next bar would begin. One bar is played twice to make two. AMENITY plays it at its own tempo, chopped into sixteen 8ths.

Drop a file here, or click to choose one

Good to know

Stop AMENITY (and any external MIDI clock) before you send or delete: writing to its memory pauses the sound for a moment. Your breaks stay when you update AMENITY; installing another firmware may remove them.

Each break is made as loud as the built-in ones (its peaks limited when it is raised) and its one-shots on the black keys are picked from it: kick, snare, hi-hat and ride. A break over 3 seconds (two bars slower than 160 BPM) is stored at a lower rate so that it fits its slot.

Only use audio you are allowed to use. What you put on your FM-1 stays on your FM-1: nothing is uploaded anywhere, everything happens in this page.
