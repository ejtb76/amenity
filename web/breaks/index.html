AMENITY breaks

Put your own breaks on your FM-1: four slots, after the built-in ones on PRESET. Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB and running AMENITY 2 or later.

FM-1

Not connected.

Your breaks

Drop a file of exactly one or two bars on a slot (starting on the first beat, ending where the next bar would begin; one bar is played twice). Drag a slot by its handle to change the order PRESET plays them in.

Connect to see them.

Good to know

Stop AMENITY (and any external MIDI clock) before you send, delete or reorder: writing to its memory pauses the sound for a moment. Your breaks stay when you update AMENITY; installing another firmware may remove them.

Each break is made as loud as the built-in ones (its peaks limited when it is raised) and its one-shots on the black keys are picked from it: kick, snare, hi-hat and ride. A break over 3 seconds (two bars slower than 160 BPM) is stored at a lower rate so that it fits its slot.

Only use audio you are allowed to use. What you put on your FM-1 stays on your FM-1: nothing is uploaded anywhere, everything happens in this page.
